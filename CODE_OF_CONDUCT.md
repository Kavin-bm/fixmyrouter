# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short:

- Be kind and patient. Many people here are not network engineers, and "my parents' router" questions are welcome.
- Assume good faith, give constructive feedback, and accept it gracefully.
- No harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, sexualised language, or publishing others' private information (including someone's network details).
- Don't use this project, its issues or its profiles to help attack networks you don't own.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently ban contributors who break these rules.

**Reporting:** contact the maintainers privately via the repository's **Security → Report a vulnerability** form (it reaches maintainers only), or by direct message to a maintainer. Reports are handled confidentially.
